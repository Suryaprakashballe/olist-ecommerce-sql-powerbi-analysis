📊 Olist E-Commerce Business Performance Dashboard
🧭 Dashboard Overview

This dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of the Brazilian Olist e-commerce platform, focusing on revenue performance, customer behavior, seller contribution, product categories, payment methods, and logistics impact.
All metrics are calculated using delivered orders only to ensure business accuracy.

🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Revenue: R$ 19.77M

Total Orders: 96,455 delivered orders

Average Order Value (AOV): R$ 204.99

Total Unique Customers: 93,342 (One-time: 90,542 + Repeat: 2,800 — see Customer Behavior section below)

> ⚠️ **Correction:** an earlier version of this README listed "Total Customers" as
> 96,455 — the same as Total Orders. That number was computed on `customer_id`, which
> in the Olist schema is generated **per order**, not per person. The true unique
> customer count uses `customer_unique_id` (as the CLV queries in `sql/03_customer_lifetime_value_analysis.sql`
> correctly do), giving 93,342 — not 96,455. This is a common gotcha with this dataset
> and worth knowing cold if asked about it.

These KPIs summarize the overall health and scale of marketplace activity.

📈 Revenue Trend Analysis

The monthly revenue trend shows consistent growth over time, reflecting increasing platform adoption.
Seasonal spikes are visible in certain months, indicating periodic demand surges and promotional effects.

Insight:
Revenue closely follows order volume, suggesting a stable average order value across the analysis period.

💳 Payment Method Analysis

Credit cards are the dominant payment method, contributing approximately 77% of total revenue, followed by boleto and debit cards.

Business Insight:
Customers strongly prefer flexible payment options, making credit card support critical for revenue growth.

🛍️ Product Category Performance

Revenue is concentrated in a small set of categories such as:

cama_mesa_banho

beleza_saude

informatica_acessorios

However, categories with the highest order volume are not always the highest revenue contributors, highlighting pricing and margin differences.

⭐ Premium Category Identification (High AOV)

Certain categories exhibit significantly higher Average Order Value:

pcs

telefonia_fixa

portateis_casa_forno_e_cafe

Insight:
These premium categories present strong opportunities for upselling and targeted marketing, despite lower order volumes.

🧑‍💼 Seller Performance Analysis

A small group of sellers generates a disproportionately large share of revenue, indicating seller dependency within the marketplace.

Key Observation:
Some sellers achieve high revenue with fewer orders (high-value products), while others rely on high order volume with lower revenue per order.

🚚 Freight & Logistics Impact

Freight cost forms a significant portion of product pricing:

Average freight cost: R$ 20

Average product price: R$ 120

Certain categories show very high freight-to-price ratios, which may negatively affect conversion rates.

Actionable Insight:
Logistics optimization and bundled pricing could improve customer satisfaction and sales in freight-heavy categories.

👥 Customer Behavior Analysis

One-time Customers: 90,542

Repeat Customers: 2,800

Although repeat customers are fewer, they contribute a higher average lifetime value.

Key Metrics:

Avg CLV (One-time): R$ 160.77

Avg CLV (Repeat): R$ 308.59

Insight:
Customer retention represents a major growth opportunity, as repeat customers generate nearly 2× higher lifetime value.

🧠 Key Business Takeaways

Customer retention is a critical growth lever.

Revenue is highly concentrated across specific sellers, products, and categories.

Premium categories and high-CLV customers offer high ROI targeting opportunities.

Freight cost optimization can significantly improve purchasing decisions and profitability.
